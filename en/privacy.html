Privacy
Policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you contact us through the website or by email, why we collect it and what rights you have.

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is Key Plus, an architecture and interior design studio [full legal name and tax ID, if the studio is registered as a sole trader or company]. For any question about your data, write to studiokeyplus@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

When you fill in the form on the Contact page or send us an email, we only collect what you send us yourself:

  • your full name,
  • your email address,
  • the content of your message and any details you include (for example a description or photos of your space).

3. Why we use it and on what basis

We use your data only to reply to your enquiry and, if we agree to work together, to prepare an offer and the project. We do not use it for advertising.

The legal basis is your consent, given by ticking the box in the form, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract. We process data in line with the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (Official Gazette of the Republic of Serbia, No. 87/2018) and, for visitors from the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as our communication and cooperation last, and no longer than [period, for example 2 years] after the last contact, unless the law requires a longer period. After that, we delete it.

5. Who else has access

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing. We receive messages through Gmail (Google) [and a form-sending service: service name]. These providers may store data on servers outside Serbia, under the safeguards set out in their terms.

6. Your rights

At any time you have the right to:

  • find out what data we hold about you and receive a copy,
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data,
  • ask us to delete your data,
  • ask us to restrict processing or object to it,
  • ask us to transfer your data to another controller,
  • withdraw your consent, without affecting processing carried out before the withdrawal.

Send your request to studiokeyplus@gmail.com. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can file a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of Serbia (www.poverenik.rs) or with the supervisory authority in your EU country.

7. Cookies and external services

The website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools. [If a tool such as Google Analytics is added later, this section must be updated.] Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which passes your IP address to Google. The link to our Instagram profile leads to an external website with its own privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date of the latest change is always shown at the top of this page.
